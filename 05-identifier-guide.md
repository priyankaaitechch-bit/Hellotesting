# Identifier Guide — Country Flags vs Team/Club Logos

For each subject in the prompt, you must specify a circular badge that visually identifies them. This sits in the identifier row near the top of the image.

The choice between **country flag** and **team/club logo** depends on the sport's culture and what feels most iconic for the player.

## ⚠️ IMPORTANT WARNING ABOUT LOGO ACCURACY

AI image generators (Grok, Gemini, ChatGPT image, Midjourney) **struggle with rendering team logos accurately**. Bulls logos may look distorted, Lakers wordmarks may be misspelled, etc.

**When generating prompts that include team logos:**

1. Describe the logo using its **visual elements** rather than just naming the team. Example:
   - ❌ "Chicago Bulls logo" (might come out distorted)
   - ✅ "Circular badge featuring an angry red bull face mascot logo on white background"
   
2. For the most iconic logos, provide both the name and the visual description so the AI has a fallback:
   - "Kansas City Chiefs logo — red arrowhead shape with white 'KC' letters inside"
   - "Los Angeles Lakers logo — purple and gold 'Lakers' wordmark with basketball"
   - "New England Patriots logo — navy blue Patriot warrior face in red, white, and blue"

3. Mention this in the prompt's photorealism enforcement: "no warped logos, accurate team colors and shapes".

4. **Recommend to user (in the prompt itself if needed):** if the generated logo looks off, the image can be edited in Canva to overlay the real high-resolution logo.
