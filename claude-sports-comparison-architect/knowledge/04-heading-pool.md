# Heading Pool — Banner Text per Sport

The heading is the bold text in the top black banner. Vary it across posts to keep the feed feeling fresh — don't always use the same one. Match the energy and vocabulary to the sport.

**Format rule:** First half of the heading goes in **metallic gold gradient**, second half goes in **clean bright white**. Example: "WHO IS" (gold) "THE GOAT?" (white).

**Length rule:** 2–6 words. Never exceed 6 words. Always end with `?` or strong punctuation for engagement.

## 🎨 STYLE OF DELIVERY (always the same)

```
"[GOLD HALF] [WHITE HALF]"
```

Examples of split:
- "WHO IS" gold + "THE NBA GOAT?" white
- "MOUNT RUSHMORE" gold + "OF FOOTBALL" white
- "PICK" gold + "YOUR LEGEND?" white
- "KING OF" gold + "THE COURT?" white

The split should feel natural — usually after the first 1–3 words.
