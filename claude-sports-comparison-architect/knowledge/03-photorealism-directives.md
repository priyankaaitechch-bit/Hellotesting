# Photorealism Directives — Mandatory Block

This block MUST be appended verbatim at the end of every prompt you generate. It is the single most important section for ensuring images look like real photographs and not "AI-generated".

---

## THE MANDATORY BLOCK (paste verbatim into every prompt)

```
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
HYPER-REALISM PHOTOGRAPHY DIRECTIVES (MANDATORY)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

CAMERA & LENS:
- Shot on Canon EOS R5 / Sony A7R IV / Nikon Z9 (professional 
  full-frame DSLR)
- 85mm f/1.4 prime lens for portraits, OR 70-200mm f/2.8 telephoto 
  for action shots
- ISO 400-1600 depending on lighting condition
- Cinematic shallow depth of field with creamy bokeh background

PHOTOGRAPHY GENRE:
- Sports Illustrated / GQ / SLAM / NFL Films magazine cover style
- Professional sports photojournalism by a top-tier photographer
- Editorial-grade composition with rule-of-thirds framing

SKIN & FACE REALISM (CRITICAL):
- Natural skin texture with VISIBLE PORES at every visible patch
- Real fine wrinkles, expression lines, and minor skin imperfections
- Authentic subsurface scattering (light passing through skin in 
  ears, nose, fingers)
- Real sweat sheen on forehead, neck, arms where appropriate
- Beard / stubble shows individual hair detail
- Eyes have authentic catchlights, real iris detail, slight redness 
  in inner corners
- NO airbrushed plastic skin, NO smooth doll-like surfaces, 
  NO perfect symmetry

LIGHTING REALISM:
- Real-world physically-based lighting consistent with the setting
- Subtle lens flare from strong light sources where naturally present
- Realistic cast shadows with soft penumbra
- Authentic colored ambient bounce light from surroundings (e.g., 
  green from grass, red from team jersey)

MATERIAL & FABRIC:
- Authentic fabric weave visible on jerseys and uniforms
- Real fabric wrinkles, creases, and natural drape
- Slight wear, sweat patches, dirt where appropriate to the action
- Accurate jersey number stitching, team crest embroidery detail

POST-PROCESSING / FILM FEEL:
- Subtle film grain (35mm-equivalent ISO 400 grain) for analog 
  authenticity
- Slight chromatic aberration on high-contrast edges
- Natural color grading — NOT over-saturated, NOT HDR-look

ANTI-AI ENFORCEMENT (read carefully):
- Output MUST look like an authentic real-world photograph
- Output MUST NOT look like CGI, NOT 3D render, NOT digital painting, 
  NOT illustration, NOT AI art
- NO smooth airbrushed plastic skin
- NO perfect cookie-cutter facial symmetry
- NO uncanny valley artifacts
- NO over-glossy surfaces, NO over-shiny highlights
- NO distorted hands, NO extra fingers, NO warped jersey numbers, 
  NO warped team logos, NO warped flag designs
- NO floating limbs, NO incorrect anatomy
- NO text artifacts on uniforms or backgrounds (other than the 
  intended heading and nameplates)

LIKENESS REQUIREMENT:
- Each subject's face MUST be a highly accurate likeness of the 
  named real-world athlete
- Match the era specified (prime-era look for retired legends, 
  current-day look for active players)
- Hair, build, and signature features must be unmistakably correct

OUTPUT QUALITY:
- 8K equivalent sharpness on faces and primary subjects
- Composed as a single finished poster image (not multiple panels 
  stitched obviously)
- Final image must be ready to publish on Facebook without further 
  editing other than the planned 4:5 center crop
```

---

## WHY THIS BLOCK MATTERS

Without these directives:
- AI generators default to smooth, plastic, doll-like skin
- Faces feel generic and lose specific likeness
- Output looks like 3D render or illustration, not photo
- Engagement on social media drops dramatically

With these directives, the output reaches a level where casual viewers in a Facebook feed scroll cannot immediately identify the image as AI — which is the entire goal.

**Always include this block. Never skip it. Never abbreviate it.**
