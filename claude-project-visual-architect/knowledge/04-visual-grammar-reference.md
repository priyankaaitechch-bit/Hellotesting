## CAMERA MOVEMENT (read-only lookup)

- Static / locked, push-in, pull-back, pan, tilt, handheld, tracking, whip pan, crane, zoom, orbit

## SHOT SIZES

- ECU (extreme close-up), CU (close-up), MCU (medium close-up), MS (medium), MLS (medium long), WS (wide), EWS (extreme wide), OTS (over the shoulder), two-shot, Dutch angle, low angle, high angle, bird's eye

## CUT TYPES

- Straight cut, hard cut / jump cut, smash cut, match cut, L-cut, J-cut, cross-cut, dissolve, fade, wipe, montage, freeze frame

## SPEED

- Normal, slow motion (<50%), super slow (10-25%), time-lapse, hyperlapse, speed ramp, freeze frame

## COLOR GRADES

- Teal-orange (blockbuster), desaturated, bleach bypass, warm vintage, cool noir, sepia, B&W, high-sat pop, green cast (Matrix), pastel/faded

## OVERLAYS / TEXTURES

- Film grain (8/16/35mm), dust/scratches, VHS scanlines, CRT glitch, light leaks, lens flare, halftone dots, chromatic aberration, letterbox bars, vignette, paper texture

## TYPOGRAPHY CATEGORIES

- Serif (literary/doc), sans-serif modern (clean/explainer), sans-serif bold (hype/news), monospace (tech/data), script (rare — only personal), display (title cards only)

## TRANSITIONS BY VIBE

- Dark investigative: hard cuts, occasional glitch
- Fast hype: jump cuts, smash cuts, speed ramps
- Nostalgic retro: wipes, dissolves, VHS tracking
- Clean educational: simple L-cuts
- Horror: sudden cuts, flickers, freeze frames
- Epic cinematic: slow cross-dissolves on wides, hard cuts on action
- Comic book: panel wipes, smash cuts with borders

## ASPECT RATIOS

- 16:9 (standard YouTube), 2.35:1 / 2.39:1 (cinematic letterbox), 4:3 (retro/VHS), 1:1 (social), 9:16 (vertical shorts)
