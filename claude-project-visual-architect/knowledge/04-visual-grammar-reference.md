# VISUAL GRAMMAR REFERENCE

The cinematography, editing, and post-production vocabulary you use when writing 🎬 VISUAL DIRECTION. Speak the language editors actually speak so recommendations are immediately actionable.

## CAMERA MOVEMENT

| Term | What it means | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Static / Locked | Camera does not move | Contemplative moments, revelations, emotional weight |
| Push-in | Camera moves slowly toward subject | Building tension, emphasizing emotion, key realization |
| Pull-back / Dolly-out | Camera retreats from subject | Isolation, scale reveal, ending a beat |
| Pan (left/right) | Rotates horizontally on fixed pivot | Scanning, revealing context |
| Tilt (up/down) | Rotates vertically on fixed pivot | Revealing height, shifting emphasis |
| Handheld | Organic micro-movement | Realism, unease, documentary feel |
| Tracking / Dolly | Moves alongside subject | Conversations, reveals, walking scenes |
| Whip pan | Extremely fast pan | Transition energy, topic shift |
| Crane / Jib | Vertical movement on arm | Establishing shots, dramatic reveals |
| Zoom-in / Zoom-out | Lens focal length changes (not camera position) | Emphasis without motion; vintage feel |
| Orbit | Circles around subject | Drama, isolation, 360 context |
| Snorricam / Rig-mounted | Camera locked to subject, background moves | Disorientation, psychological distress |

## SHOT COMPOSITION

| Term | Framing |
|---|---|
| Extreme Close-Up (ECU) | Just eyes, mouth, or a detail |
| Close-Up (CU) | Face fills frame |
| Medium Close-Up (MCU) | Chest up |
| Medium Shot (MS) | Waist up |
| Cowboy Shot | Mid-thigh up |
| Medium Long Shot (MLS) | Full body with headroom |
| Wide Shot (WS) | Subject visible, environment dominant |
| Extreme Wide Shot (EWS) | Subject tiny in landscape |
| Two-Shot | Two subjects in frame |
| Over-the-Shoulder (OTS) | From behind one subject to another |
| Dutch Angle | Camera tilted — unease, disorientation |
| Low Angle | Camera below subject — power, intimidation |
| High Angle | Camera above subject — vulnerability, insignificance |
| Bird's-Eye / Top-Down | Directly overhead — observational, clinical |

## EDIT / CUT TYPES

| Term | What it is |
|---|---|
| Straight cut | Standard, no transition |
| Hard cut / Jump cut | Abrupt change, often on beat |
| Smash cut | Hard cut between high-contrast tones (loud → silent, bright → dark) |
| Match cut | Cut between two visually or thematically similar frames |
| L-cut | Audio from next shot starts before the visual cuts |
| J-cut | Visual cuts before audio from next shot ends |
| Cross-cut / Parallel edit | Alternating between two scenes |
| Dissolve / Crossfade | Gradual blend (use sparingly — feels dated) |
| Fade in / Fade out | To/from black or white |
| Iris | Classic circular fade (retro/comic aesthetic only) |
| Wipe | One image pushes another off |
| Montage | Compressed time via rapid cuts |
| Freeze frame | Footage stops, typography or push-in continues |

## SPEED MANIPULATION

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| Normal speed | 100% / 24fps |
| Slow motion | <50% speed, shot at higher fps for smoothness |
| Super slow-mo | 10–25% speed |
| Time-lapse | Accelerated (e.g., 500%+) |
| Hyperlapse | Moving time-lapse |
| Speed ramp | Dynamic shift from fast to slow or vice versa within a shot |
| Time remap | Non-linear speed changes across a clip |
| Freeze frame | 0% speed, held indefinitely |

## TYPOGRAPHY ON SCREEN

### When to use typography
- Quotes from the script or from characters
- Statistics / data
- Chapter titles
- Speaker attribution
- Translations
- Emphasis on a key word

### Font categories
- **Serif** (Times, Playfair, EB Garamond) — literary, documentary, serious
- **Sans-serif modern** (Inter, Helvetica, Neue Haas) — clean explainer, tech
- **Sans-serif bold/condensed** (Impact, Bebas Neue, Oswald) — hype, sports, news
- **Monospace** (JetBrains Mono, Courier) — data, tech, investigative
- **Script / Handwritten** — rarely appropriate; use only for personal / emotional moments
- **Display / Decorative** — title cards only, never body text

### Typography treatments
- **Kinetic typography** — words appear one at a time synced to narration
- **Lower third** — name/title card bottom-left
- **Title card** — fullscreen chapter header
- **Subtitle burn-in** — translated or emphasized speech
- **Pull quote** — large centered quote with attribution
- **Scroll text** — news-ticker style
- **Typewriter effect** — letters appear one-by-one

### Typography positioning
- Rule of thirds — place text at intersections
- Safe area — stay 5% from edges for YouTube overlay
- Use weight and size to establish hierarchy
- White text on dark backgrounds = most legible on YouTube

## COMPOSITION RULES WORTH NAMING

- **Rule of thirds** — place subject on 1/3 gridlines
- **Leading lines** — natural lines draw eye to subject
- **Negative space** — emptiness around subject = loneliness, emphasis
- **Symmetry** — centered composition = formality, unease (Kubrick)
- **Frame within a frame** — doorways, windows framing subject
- **Depth of field** — foreground / midground / background layers
- **Golden ratio / Fibonacci** — alternative to rule of thirds
- **Bird's-eye / God's-eye view** — subject insignificance
- **Leading room** — space in front of a moving subject

## TRANSITIONS BY VIBE

| Vibe | Preferred transition |
|---|---|
| Dark investigative | Hard cuts on beat, occasional glitch |
| Fast-paced hype | Jump cuts, smash cuts, speed ramps |
| Nostalgic retro | Wipes, dissolves, VHS tracking |
| Educational clean | Simple L-cuts, no flashy transitions |
| Horror / creepy | Sudden cuts, freeze frames, light flickers |
| Epic / cinematic | Slow cross-dissolves on wide shots, hard cuts on action |
| Comic book | Panel wipes, smash cuts with panel borders |

## CHEAT SHEET — MOST COMMON BREAKDOWN INSTRUCTIONS

Use these phrases verbatim in 🎬 VISUAL DIRECTION — they're editor-fluent:

- `"Slow 10% zoom-in over 4 seconds"`
- `"Hard cut on beat at 0:03"`
- `"Letterbox bars in at 0:01, hold through shot"`
- `"Speed ramps from 25% to 100% on impact"`
- `"Freeze frame at 0:02, camera push-in continues, title appears"`
- `"Split screen — left: [Clip A], right: [Clip B], divided by 2px white line"`
- `"Kinetic typography, words in sync with VO, bold white on black, Inter Bold"`
- `"Ken Burns pan left-to-right over 5 seconds"`
- `"VHS scanline overlay at 15% opacity, chromatic aberration at shot start"`
- `"Vignette at 40%, saturation -30%, hold 4 seconds"`
- `"Picture-in-picture — main clip full frame, smaller clip inset bottom-right at 28%"`
- `"Halftone dot overlay at 20%, high contrast, black panel borders"`
