# YOUTUBE SOURCING DATABASE

Authoritative list of channels and source ecosystems for video essay asset hunting. Organized by content type. Use this when recommending "channels to search directly" in breakdown blocks.

## VIDEO GAMES — Cutscenes, Cinematics, Gameplay

### Tier 1 — Primary sources
- **@GameMovies** — Full-length "game movies" (all cutscenes stitched), most AAA titles, 1080p/4K
- **@ZanarAesthetics** — 4K cinematic cutscene compilations, heavy PS4/PS5 focus, high aesthetic quality
- **@CutscenesCentral** — Older and retro game cutscene archives
- **@BossFightDatabase** — Boss fights + story sequences across decades of games

### Tier 2 — Official studio channels
- **@PlayStation** — Official Sony first-party clips, trailers, launch cinematics
- **@Xbox** — Official Microsoft / Xbox Game Studios content
- **@NintendoAmerica** — Official Nintendo clips
- **@NaughtyDog** — The Last of Us / Uncharted official clips
- **@RockstarGames** — GTA / Red Dead / Max Payne official content
- **@FromSoftware** — Souls series / Elden Ring / Sekiro trailers
- **@CDPROJEKTRED** — Witcher / Cyberpunk 2077

### Tier 3 — Niche / specific
- **@TheMediaKnightOfficial** — Cutscenes + analysis
- **@SlyFlySays** — Gameplay walkthroughs with clean audio
- **@Shirrako** — Mission walkthroughs, especially Rockstar games

### Search anatomy for games
```
[Game Title] all cutscenes [4K / 1080p] [no commentary]
[Game Title] [Character Name] all scenes
[Game Title] [chapter / mission name] cutscene
[Game Title] full story movie [year of release]
```

## AI VIDEO GENERATION (FALLBACK OF LAST RESORT)

When a scene is genuinely impossible to source:
- **Runway Gen-3 Alpha** — Best for cinematic motion, short clips
- **Kling AI** — Strong motion, longer clips
- **Google Veo 3** — High fidelity, integrates with Vertex AI
- **Minimax (Hailuo)** — Fast iteration
- **Luma Dream Machine** — Good for abstract motion
- **Pika Labs** — Faster turnaround, lower fidelity

Prompt anatomy for AI video:
`[shot type] + [camera movement] + [subject description with clothing/action] + [lighting] + [setting/background] + [art style or film reference] + [mood] + [aspect ratio] + [duration]`

## META-SEARCH STRATEGIES (when a specific clip is hard to find)

1. **Reverse image search** — Screenshot a known frame from the source, reverse-image-search on Google Images → often leads to YouTube embeds
2. **Reddit community crowdsourcing** — r/scenepacks, r/animeedit, r/editors, show-specific subs
3. **Twitter/X video search** — Use `search_query filter:native_video` syntax
4. **Facebook video search** — Public pages often host rare clips
5. **Vimeo** — Higher-quality film school uploads, student essays, trailers
6. **Dailymotion** — European mirror of many takedown-targeted uploads
7. **Telegram channels** — Scene-pack Telegram channels exist for most major shows (search in Telegram directly)
