# 05 — GENRE PLAYBOOKS

One playbook per niche. CLIP HUNTER picks the matching playbook from Phase 1 and applies it silently. Do not dump these contents into user replies.

---

## HOLLYWOOD FILM (MODERN, POST-1980)

**First stops:** @MOVIECLIPS (licensed), studio channels (@SonyPictures, @warnerbrospictures, @UniversalPictures, @DisneyStudios, @A24, @ParamountPictures, @Lionsgate, @FocusFeatures, @NEONrated), fan scene packs

**Query recipes:**
- `Movieclips [Film] [scene]`
- `[Film] [character] [action/emotion] scene 1080p`
- `[Film] scene pack 1080p`
- `[Film] "[iconic line]"` (quoted phrase)

**Stills:** Film Grab (film-grab.com), TMDB media tab, IMDb photos, Reddit r/MovieDetails

**Gotchas:**
- Studio clips often geo-blocked — fan channels are backup
- A24 / indie studios have sparser YouTube presence — lean on trailers + interviews
- Movieclips often uses shortened clips (30-60s) — use fan packs for full scenes

---

## CLASSIC / OLD CINEMA (PRE-1980)

**First stops:** archive.org (pre-1965 public domain films often hosted in full), @TCM, @CriterionCollection, studio vault channels (@warnerclassics, @ClassicHollywood-FilmCinema), fan uploaded retro channels

**Query recipes:**
- `[Film Title] [Year] full movie` (may return complete film on archive.org)
- `[Film] [Year] [famous scene] classic cinema`
- `[Director Name] [Film] scene analysis`
- `[Actor Name] [Film] [Year] best scenes`

**Stills:** Wikimedia Commons (strong for pre-1960), Library of Congress, Getty Images (licensed), doctormacro.com, Film Grab for canonical classics

**Gotchas:**
- Pre-1930 films often silent — plan sound design around music + Foley
- B&W quality varies wildly — specify "HD remaster" or "restored" in queries
- Some classics are only on TCM / Criterion Channel — YouTube may only have trailers

## TV SHOW

**First stops:** Network official channels (@HBO, @Netflix, @PrimeVideo, @AMC_TV, @AppleTV, @FXNetworks, @Showtime, @hulu), show-specific fan clip channels (search `[Show] clips` or `[Show] scenes`)

**Query recipes:**
- `[Show] S[X]E[Y] [Character] [scene]`
- `[Show] "[iconic line]"`
- `[Show] [character] all scenes 1080p`
- `[Show] best moments [character]`

**Stills:** Film Grab (major shows), ScreenMusings.org (comprehensive screencaps), TMDB, show subreddits

**Gotchas:**
- Prestige dramas (HBO, AMC) have the best clip ecosystems
- Reality TV + talk shows need official network channels (rarely fan-uploaded)
- Anthology shows (Black Mirror, AHS) — specify season + episode

---

## ANIME

**First stops:** @Crunchyroll, @ToeiAnimationOfficial, @mappa_info, Bilibili.com (massive fan mirror), Niconico, twixtor/edit compilation channels on YouTube

**Query recipes:**
- `[Anime] [Character] [scene type] raw 1080p` ("raw" = no subs)
- `[Anime] episode [#] [scene] clip`
- `[Anime] twixtor [character] 4K`
- `[Character] [Anime] best moments 4K`
- `[Anime] [scene] 4K upscale`

**Stills:** MyAnimeList promo tab, Zerochan, AniDB, Pinterest (verify resolution)

**Gotchas:**
- Takedowns are constant — 20%+ of clip search results will be dead links
- "Raw" in anime = no subtitles (different from Western use)
- Dub vs sub — specify if the essay cares
- Bilibili is critical for obscure scenes — learn to search its interface

## CARTOON (WESTERN ANIMATION)

**First stops:** Official studio channels (@CartoonNetwork, @DisneyChannel, @Nickelodeon, @AdultSwim, @NetflixAnime if cartoon series), @PBS (for retro educational cartoons), fan compilation channels per show

**Query recipes:**
- `[Cartoon Series] [Character] [scene/episode] clip`
- `[Cartoon] S[X]E[Y] [moment]`
- `[Cartoon] best moments [character] 1080p`
- `[Retro cartoon] full episode [episode title]`

**Stills:** Fandom wikis (each major cartoon has one with screencap galleries), DeviantArt fan art, official press kits

**Gotchas:**
- Older cartoons (pre-2000) often have variable upload quality — search "HD" or "remastered"
- Adult Swim content has its own dedicated fan ecosystem
- Disney is aggressive about takedowns — lean on official clips + trailers

## VIDEO GAME

**First stops:** @GameMovies (the #1 source for game essays), @ZanarAesthetics (4K cutscene quality), @CutscenesCentral (older games), @BossFightDatabase, official dev channels (@NaughtyDog, @RockstarGames, @FromSoftware, @CDPROJEKTRED, @PlayStation, @Xbox, @NintendoAmerica)

**Query recipes:**
- `[Game] all cutscenes 4K no commentary`
- `[Game] [Chapter/Mission] cutscene`
- `[Game] [Character] all scenes`
- `[Game] [Mission] walkthrough no commentary 4K`

**Stills:** MobyGames (comprehensive), Steam store pages, press kits (`[Game] press kit`), ArtStation (concept art)

**Gotchas:**
- Older games (pre-2010) may only have 720p cutscenes
- Cross-platform games have different visuals — specify platform if it matters (PC vs PS5)
- Speedrunners often have cleanest gameplay footage — search `[Game] speedrun no commentary`

## DOCUMENTARY / HISTORICAL

**First stops:** @britishpathe (pre-1970 global), @APArchive (1895–present), @Reuters, @CriticalPast (WWII+), @USNationalArchives, @NASA, @CSPAN (US political), @ImperialWarMuseums, @LibraryOfCongress, @dwdocumentary, @JourneymanPictures

**Query recipes:**
- `[Event] [Year] archival footage`
- `[Person] [Year] original speech`
- `British Pathé [topic]`
- `AP Archive [topic] [year]`
- `[Event] contemporary news coverage [year]`

**Stills:** Wikimedia Commons, Library of Congress Digital Collections, Getty (paid), AP Images (paid), Europeana

**Gotchas:**
- Pre-1910 events have few motion sources — substitute with photos + maps + typography
- Post-training recent events need fresh search — flag as low-confidence
- Some archival footage is silent — plan sound design around narration + Foley

## TRUE CRIME

**First stops:** @ABCNews, @NBCNews, @FOXnews, @CBSNews, @datelineNBC, @20_20, Wayback Machine for defunct websites/forums, court records (PACER, state systems)

**Query recipes:**
- `[Case Name] news coverage [year]`
- `[Victim/Subject] news report`
- `[Location] exterior night footage`
- `[Forum/Website] screen recording [topic]`

**Stills:** News archive websites, local news station sites, Find A Grave (handle with respect), court document screenshots

**Gotchas:**
- Respect real victims — avoid gratuitous imagery
- Verify screenshots — online mystery communities propagate false images
- Post-training recent cases need live search

## FINANCE / BUSINESS

**First stops:** @Bloomberg, @CNBC, @Reuters, @FinancialTimes, @TheEconomist, @Forbes, @TED, @CSPAN (US hearings — SBF, Zuckerberg testimony), stock footage platforms

**Query recipes:**
- `[Company] offices b-roll`
- `[CEO] interview [year]`
- `[Topic] CNBC coverage`
- `[Event] Bloomberg footage`
- `[Concept] motion graphics animation`

**Stills:** Company logos (Wikimedia + Brandfetch), CEO portraits (Wikipedia + Getty), charts (TradingView screenshots, FRED for historical)

**Stock b-roll:** Pexels, Artgrid (paid), Envato Elements (paid), Coverr, Mixkit

**Gotchas:**
- Crypto content ages fast — flag post-training topics
- "Money falling" stock is overused — look for unusual angles
- Charts must be legible at YouTube 1080p

## SCIENCE / TECH

**First stops:** @NASA, @CERN, @SciShow, @Veritasium, @smartereveryday, @TED, @Vsauce, @Kurzgesagt, university channels (MIT OCW, Stanford)

**Query recipes:**
- `[Discovery/Concept] [Institution] explainer`
- `[Experiment] demonstration footage`
- `[Mission/Launch] NASA footage`
- `[Scientific concept] animation`

**Stills:** NASA Image Gallery, CERN Document Server, ESO (European Southern Observatory), Wikimedia Commons Science

**Gotchas:**
- Abstract concepts need motion graphics — suggest Flourish, After Effects templates
- Recent discoveries may be post-training — live search critical
- Peer-review sensitivity — avoid misrepresenting studies

## SPORTS

**First stops:** Official league channels (@NBA, @NFL, @FIFA, @UEFA, @premierleague, @MLB, @F1), @ESPN, team official channels, highlight compilation channels

**Query recipes:**
- `[Player] [Match/Event] highlights`
- `[Team] vs [Team] [Year] full game highlights`
- `[Moment/Play] [Year] footage`

**Stills:** Getty Sports (paid), team official sites, Wikimedia for historic athletes

**Gotchas:**
- League copyright enforcement is aggressive — stick to official + fair use commentary
- Highlight reels get compressed — specify "4K" or "official" in queries

## SCI-FI RETROSPECTIVE

**First stops:** Movieclips, studio vault channels, fan edit/twixtor channels (search `[Franchise] twixtor`), concept art compilations

**Query recipes:**
- `[Film] [scene] 4K remaster`
- `[Franchise] behind the scenes [Year]`
- `[Franchise] concept art compilation`
- `[Franchise] fan edit cinematic`

**Stills:** Film Grab, franchise-specific wikis (Wookieepedia for Star Wars, Memory Alpha for Star Trek), ArtStation concept art, official press kits

**Gotchas:**
- Major franchises have decades of material — specify era (OT vs prequels vs sequels)
- Concept art is a huge asset class for sci-fi essays — lean on it heavily
- Visual effects breakdowns often live on VFX-studio YouTube channels (ILM, Weta, DNEG)

## INTERNET / MEME CULTURE

**First stops:** KnowYourMeme.com, Reddit top posts (r/OutOfTheLoop, r/meme history), Wayback Machine, YouTube meme compilation channels, Vine archives (@BestOfVineOfficial)

**Query recipes:**
- `[Meme name] origin video`
- `[Meme] compilation [year]`
- `[Viral moment] original clip`

**Stills:** KnowYourMeme entries, Reddit screenshots, Wayback Machine captures

**Gotchas:**
- Many OG meme videos are deleted — Wayback Machine is essential
- Source attribution matters — give credit to original uploaders
- Some memes age poorly — verify current cultural context

---

## CROSS-GENRE SCRIPTS

If a script crosses genres (e.g., a game essay with a historical analogy, or a film retrospective with financial context), apply **both** playbooks to the relevant sections. Flag the switch silently — no need to announce it to the user.
