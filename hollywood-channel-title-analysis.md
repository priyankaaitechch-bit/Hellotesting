# Hollywood Channel — Master Title Format Analysis & Strategy
# (v2 — Synthesized from 4 independent AI analyses + data review)

> This is the consolidated strategy document combining analysis from Kiro, ChatGPT, Grok, and Gemini on the same dataset of 20+ competitor channels. Only patterns confirmed across multiple AI analyses AND with strong data signal are included.

---

## HOW TO READ THIS DOCUMENT

- **VPH** = Views Per Hour (velocity metric — most honest indicator)
- **Multiplier** = how much video over-performed vs channel average
- **Cross-AI confidence** = how many of 4 analyses flagged this format as top-tier
- **TIER S** = 4/4 AI consensus + strong data. Build channel around these.
- **TIER A** = 3/4 AI consensus + strong data. Reliable.
- **TIER B** = Niche winners, situational.
- **TIER C** = Avoid / oversaturated / declining.

# 🎯 TIER B — SITUATIONAL / NICHE

### 13. `Most Loved VS Most Hated [X]` / Polarizing Tribalism
- Data from commentary channels: 1M–3.6M views when executed on tribal topics
- **Hollywood adaptation:** "Most Loved VS Most Hated MCU Villains", "Most Loved VS Most Hated Breaking Bad Moments"
- Works IF there's genuine audience division

- 7 Levels of Video Game Developer Reputation: 229k

- 7 Levels of Disney Villains: 71k

### 15. `A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms S1E## Explained` / Weekly Recap
- 517k–1M per episode during run
- **Strategy:** Latch onto trending show from episode 1, be consistent.

### 16. `Top [N] [Adjective] [Decisions/Moments] in [Franchise]` / Listicle
- Top 12 Smartest Decisions in Horror Movies: 71k
- Top 24 Horror Movie Plot Twists: 157k, 13 VPH

# ❌ TIER C — AVOID

- **Generic "Movie Explained" videos** — oversaturated
- **Simple reviews/reactions** without analytical twist
- **Character biographies** without a hook/angle
- **Titles >70 characters** — hurts CTR and search
- **Generic "Top 10" lists** without emotional framing
- **Recap videos for non-trending shows**
- **Saw/Jason/classic horror** death compilations — saturation evident

# ⚡ POWER WORDS THAT BOOST CTR (from data)

**Impact:** DESTROYED, BROKE, HACKED, OUTSMARTED, WEAPONIZED
**Intensity:** MASTERCLASS, GENIUS, TERRIFYING, INSANE, BRUTAL, PAINFUL
**Mystery:** HIDDEN, SECRET, REAL, ACTUALLY, TRUTH
**Authority:** PROVES, EXPLAINS, MASTERPIECE, MATERMIND
**Finality:** FOREVER, EVERYTHING, ZERO CHANCE, ISN'T EVEN CLOSE

---

# 🎨 THUMBNAIL RULES (From winners in data)

1. **One character face** — shocked/intense expression
2. **Bold text overlay** — 1–3 words max (GENIUS, BROKE, NEVER, DESTROYED)
3. **High contrast colors** — red/yellow text on dark background
4. **Split-screen** — for "VS" format videos
5. **Arrow/circle highlight** — for "hidden detail" format
6. **NO AI-looking art** — real frame screenshots dominate
